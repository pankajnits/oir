2. Paste these fields
---------------------
Title:
When Can Language Models Join Without Relation Names? Unique-Path Traversal versus End-to-End Accuracy under Path Ambiguity

Keywords (comma-separated):
knowledge graphs, language models, multi-hop reasoning, evaluation, opaque identifiers, path ambiguity, abstention

TL;DR (if the form has it):
Unique sealed routes still join (OpenAI 200/200). Under two competing sealed routes, named-arm OpenAI usually answers UNKNOWN (38/200 gold, 151 UNKNOWN, 11 decoy); hashed case ids are 134/200. Entity names remain; we score the answer node, not (r1, r2).

Abstract (one paragraph, same as the PDF):
We ask whether 2-hop answering over small labeled graphs needs readable relation names, or only equality among identifiers. Opaque isomorphic reasoning (OIR) replaces relation strings with keyed opaque tokens, withholds a legend, and scores the answer node rather than an emitted (r1, r2). HMAC-SHA256 is one generator of that renaming on each instance's finite atom set, not a cryptographic or privacy primitive. Unique sealed routes still join (OpenAI unique English/opaque 200/200; hashed-id no-ARM 200/200 at 512 tokens; opaque unique 32/32 with the start in q; both-opaque unique 32/32 on three families). Under two competing sealed routes, a prompt that names the condition and uses readable case ids (named-arm) usually yields UNKNOWN, not the decoy: OpenAI 38/200 gold, UNKNOWN 151, decoy 11. The same graphs with hashed case ids (ARM line kept) are 134/200 (UNKNOWN 44, decoy 20). Entity names stay English and we score the answer node, not (r1, r2), so two-path exact-match cannot be attributed to a join over CONTEXT. Dropping the condition header, at 4096 tokens and reasoning effort medium, cyclic-decoy English two-path is 197/200 and opaque two-path is 174/200. English relation names restore OpenAI with anonymous entities (opaque-entity two-path 32/32 vs. both-opaque 1/32). On the primary 2x2, seals hide relation strings; entity names remain. They are not a confidentiality proof.

4. During review
----------------
Do not edit the author list.
You may revise the PDF after reviews appear. Keep it anonymous.

If you later post a named PDF (Zenodo), that is allowed; reviewers are
told not to hunt it. The TMLR file itself must stay anonymous.

5. After a decision
-------------------
Accept / accept with minor revision:
  Follow CAMERA_READY_AUTHOR.txt. Switch to \usepackage[accepted]{tmlr},
  put your name back, set the OpenReview URL, month, and year. The AE
  checks the camera-ready. That named file is the publication
  (ISSN 2835-8856).

Reject:
  You may resubmit a new entry with a link to the old forum and a
  changelog. Do not unmask on the rejected page if you want to resubmit;
  unmasking blocks resubmission of that work.
